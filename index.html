COMPANION QUICK-START GUIDE

Win Fanorona in Assassin's Creed IV — Zero Rules Needed

Look at the Fanorona board on your TV/monitor in Assassin's Creed IV and pick the two settings that match your screen:

1. What color are YOUR pieces at the bottom of the screen (closest to you)?
2. Who is moving first in the game right now?

When it's YOUR TURN, you don't need to know Fanorona rules — just copy the gold instructions onto your TV:

1
Find the Pulsing Gold Piece on Your TV

Because AC4's wooden board has no A–I labels, 🧭 Guided Mode tells you the exact spot in plain English relative to the center (e.g., "2nd row from bottom, 1 right of center").

2
Follow the Numbered Gold Arrows (1 → 2 → 3)

On multi-jump turns, use the Prev Hop / Next Hop buttons to highlight one jump at a time so you never lose your place.

!
What if AC4 Pauses & Asks Which Pieces to Capture?

Sometimes moving a piece can capture enemy pieces both in front of you and behind you. When that happens, we show a gold warning telling you which to pick in AC4:

  • APPROACH (Towards): Pick the enemy pieces IN FRONT of where you landed.
  • WITHDRAWAL (Away): Pick the enemy pieces BEHIND where you started.

After you click "✓ I Played This Move", watch what the Tavern AI does on your TV and record it in either of two ways:

A
1-Click Quick List (Fastest for Multi-Jumps!)

Click the blue-ringed AI piece on our board that moved on your TV. That filters the right-hand list to only that piece's moves — then just click Apply on the path that matches!

B
Step-by-Step Board Clicking

Or click the AI piece on the board, then click each empty spot it jumped to one by one. If the AI stops jumping early, click "End AI Turn Here".

Made a Mistake or Joined a Game Mid-Match?
